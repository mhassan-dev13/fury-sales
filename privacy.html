Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it does not, and how affiliate links and cookies work.

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Check price on Amazon before buying, as prices and availability change. Read the full disclosure.

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Fury Sales is a product guide and affiliate site. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, no checkout and no comments section, and it does not sell products directly. Purchases are completed on Amazon. This page explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you use this site.

Information we collect

The only personal information this site receives is what you choose to send through the Contact form: your name, your email address and your message. Contact form messages are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, and are kept only so we can reply to you and fix the issue you raised, such as a broken link or a wrong description. We do not collect payment details, and you should never send them.

We do not sell your data

Your personal information is never sold, rented or traded to anyone. It is not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and Amazon affiliate links

This site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. When you click a link to Amazon, Amazon may place cookies on your device under its own privacy policy. Those cookies let Amazon recognise that you arrived from this site, so a qualifying purchase can be credited to this site, and they also help Amazon run its store. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the guides on this site will still work, but a purchase may not be credited to this site.

Third-party services

  • Amazon: product pages, prices, checkout, delivery and returns are handled by Amazon under Amazon’s own privacy policy.
  • FormSubmit: delivers contact form messages to our email inbox. It processes the name, email and message you submit for that delivery.

Your choices

If you sent a message through the contact form and want it deleted, write again through the same form and ask, and it will be removed from the inbox. You can also browse this site without sending any personal information at all.

Children

This site is written for adult shoppers. Toy and game pages describe products for children, but purchases are made by adults on Amazon.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
